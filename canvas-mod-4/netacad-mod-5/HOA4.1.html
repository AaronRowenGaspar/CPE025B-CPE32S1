Project 1

This DIY electronics project features a paper-covered "piano" interface where tactile buttons on a breadboard are used to trigger sound through a small speaker. A microcontroller and various jumper wires coordinate the signals, creating a functional, compact synthesizer.

Project 2

The image shows a primitive line-following robot constructed with a wooden chassis, utilizing a breadboard circuit and a 9V battery. It uses infrared sensors mounted at the front to detect and navigate along a white track.

Project 3

This advanced robotic vehicle features a high-definition camera mounted on a pan-tilt bracket for computer vision tasks and environmental mapping. It is built on a professional four-wheel chassis with a complex control board, designed for sophisticated autonomous navigation and object recognition.
